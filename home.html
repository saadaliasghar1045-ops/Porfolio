Biography

A student curious about how machines learn.

I'm an ICS student at PGC. My interest in machine learning grew through Andrew Ng's courses, which introduced me to the mathematics and ideas behind models that learn from data.

Along the way, I taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and its data libraries, then explored FastAPI. I learn by experimenting, solving problems and turning concepts into small working projects.

Today, I’m strengthening my foundations in machine learning, deep learning and reinforcement learning while practising the data preparation and model-building work behind useful applications.
